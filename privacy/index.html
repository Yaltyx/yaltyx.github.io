Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Dreamforge Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Dreamforge Games YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses: through Google's YouTube APIs it uploads videos, updates channel branding, posts and manages comments on the channel's own videos, and reads channel statistics and analytics.

What it stores: only the OAuth token for the owner's own channel, kept on a private server. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of anyone else.

Google API data: use of data from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

See also the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
